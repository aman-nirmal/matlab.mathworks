Introduction to Solving Ordinary Differential Equations

Learn the fundamentals of solving first-order ordinary differential equations (ODEs) using MATLAB. Write ODE functions, apply initial conditions, and use ODE solvers to obtain and visualize numerical solutions. By the end of the course, you'll be able to solve a variety of real-world problems modeled by first-order ODEs.
